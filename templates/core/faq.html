Frequently asked questions

How does {{ SITE_NAME }} work?

Create an account, add funds to your wallet with crypto, choose a country and a service, and you receive a temporary number. The SMS code appears live on your order page.

What if my code does not arrive?

Cancel the order from its page and the full price returns to your wallet. Orders that get no code before the timer ends are refunded automatically.

Is each number single-use?

Activation numbers are for one verification. Shortly after a purchase you can re-use the same number for free from the order page (limited window, supported services only). For repeat codes over days, use Rent.

How do I add funds?

Open your wallet, enter an amount in USD, and pay with the crypto of your choice. Your balance updates automatically once the payment is confirmed on the network.

Which currencies are shown?

Every price is shown in US dollars with the Pakistani rupee equivalent next to it. Your wallet balance is held in USD.

What is the difference between buying and renting?

Buying gives a single-use number for one code. Renting keeps a private number for several days so you can receive repeat codes.

Is it safe and private?

Yes. We never ask for your personal SIM or phone number. Follow local laws and each platform's terms when using verification numbers.
